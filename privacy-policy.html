本政策適用於 Taki Hor 提供的「山野足跡 / HikeHK」iOS 及 Android App（包括 App Store、Google Play 及 TestFlight／測試版本）。英國英文商店名稱為「Hiking Routes & Offline Maps」。私隱及資料查詢請電郵 takii.hor@gmail.com。

此 App 無需帳戶，沒有廣告或第三方行為分析 SDK。路線資料只供瀏覽，尚未實地核實，路線導航保持關閉。正式安全後台及聯絡人逾時通知服務未啟用。

位置、軌跡及本機資料

取得你的同意及系統位置權限後，App 可在地圖顯示位置，並在你主動開始記錄時處理 GPS 座標、時間、精確度、速度、海拔及記錄統計。進行中的記錄可在 App 轉到背景或螢幕關閉時繼續定位。在 Android，記錄期間會顯示常駐通知，並以前台服務維持定位；App 使用 Google Play 服務的位置提供者在裝置上取得定位，Google 可能按你的裝置設定及其政策處理相關資料。你可以暫停或結束記錄，並隨時在 iOS 或 Android 設定更改位置權限；拒絕權限後仍可瀏覽路線。

軌跡、匯入的 GPX、私人標記及備註、收藏、偏好和手動公告筆記保存在裝置的 App 儲存空間。部分本機安全設定或綁定資料可保存在 iOS Keychain 或 Android Keystore。此版本沒有由我們營運的軌跡雲端同步，亦不會把完整軌跡自動上傳給開發者。

聯絡人及安全功能

iOS 的本機提醒只把你選擇的提醒時間及通知文字交給此裝置的系統通知服務，不要求聯絡人、不傳送位置，也不會確認你是否安全。你可在 App 內更改或取消提醒。通知設定、專注模式及裝置關機可能影響送達。

Android 的安全行程及開發測試畫面可讓你自行輸入或透過系統聯絡人選擇器選取一個電話號碼；App 不會讀取或上傳整個通訊錄。此版本沒有已啟用的正式安全後台，不會通知聯絡人。建立本機計劃或排入離線要求，不代表服務已接受行程或聯絡人已收到通知。請勿依賴此 App 提供救援或通知送達保證；App 不會自動報警。

若日後啟用遠端行程分享或安全服務，我們會在啟用前更新本政策，說明供應商、處理資料、所在地、保留安排及選擇，並按需要取得同意。

網絡請求及第三方服務

App 內置概覽及六區詳細地圖。使用天氣、交通或外部連結等需連線功能時，對應的官方資料服務或網站會收到正常網絡請求，包括 IP 位址、所要求的資料及一般技術資訊。這些服務按其各自的政策處理請求。我們沒有加入廣告追蹤、跨 App 追蹤或第三方行為分析 SDK。

本政策頁由 GitHub Pages 託管。GitHub 可能按其私隱聲明處理到訪這個網站的技術資訊。此頁沒有額外加入分析工具、廣告、追蹤像素或 Cookie。

你選擇的分享、應用程式商店及回報

匯出 GPX、圖片或求助文字時，資料可能包含位置、時間及你明確選擇包括的私人標記。請先檢查內容及隱藏軌跡兩端等私隱選項；接收資料的 App 或人士由你選擇，並按其政策處理。分享資料可能被接收者另存。

Apple（App Store／TestFlight）及 Google（Google Play）處理下載、安裝及更新，並可能按你的裝置設定向開發者提供彙總的下載、當機及診斷資料；請參閱 Apple 的 TestFlight 與私隱說明及 Google 私隱權政策。App 本身沒有加入當機報告或分析 SDK。你透過電郵、TestFlight 或商店評論主動提供的資料只用於支援、調查問題及改善此 App；請避免在回報中附上不必要的私人位置或其他人的資料。

保留、刪除及你的選擇

本機記錄會保留至你在 App 中刪除，或按 iOS／Android 的 App 資料管理方式移除（例如刪除 App 或清除儲存空間）。已匯出的檔案、接收者保存的副本、系統備份及 Keychain 資料可能有不同的保存方式；刪除 App 內的記錄不會同時刪除這些副本。裝置備份與還原由你的 Apple/iOS 或 Google/Android 設定控制；Android 的系統備份只包括 App 資料庫及顯示偏好。

English

This policy covers the 山野足跡 / HikeHK apps for iOS and Android, provided by Taki Hor, including App Store, Google Play and TestFlight/test builds. Its UK English store name is “Hiking Routes & Offline Maps”. For privacy and data requests, contact takii.hor@gmail.com.

No account is required. The app contains no advertising or third-party behavioural analytics SDK. Route previews are not field verified and route navigation remains disabled. A production safety backend and overdue contact notifications are not enabled.

Location, tracks and on-device information

With your consent and system location permission, the app can show your position on a map and process GPS coordinates, time, accuracy, speed, altitude and recording statistics when you start a recording. An active recording can continue using location in the background or with the screen off. On Android, recording shows an ongoing notification and uses a foreground service to keep location active; the app obtains fixes on the device through the Google Play services location provider, and Google may process related data under your device settings and its own policies. You can pause or finish recording and change location permission in iOS or Android Settings. Browsing remains available without location permission.

Tracks, imported GPX files, private markers and notes, favourites, preferences and manual notice notes are stored in the app’s on-device storage. Some local safety settings or binding information can be stored in iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. This version has no developer-operated track cloud sync and does not automatically upload your complete tracks to the developer.

Contacts and safety features

The iOS on-device reminder gives only your chosen reminder time and notification text to the device's system notification service. It needs no contact, sends no location and does not check that you are safe. You can change or cancel it in the app. Notification settings, Focus or a powered-off device can affect delivery.

Android safety plans and development test screens let you enter one phone number or select one through the system contact picker. The app does not read or upload your entire address book. This version has no enabled production safety backend and does not notify your contact. Creating a local plan or queuing an offline request does not establish server acceptance or notification delivery. Do not rely on the app for rescue or guaranteed delivery of notifications. The app does not automatically call emergency services.

Before enabling a remote trip-sharing or safety service in a later version, we will update this policy with the providers, data processed, processing locations, retention arrangements and choices, and obtain consent where needed.

Network requests and third-party services

The app bundles an overview map and detailed previews for six regions. Connected features such as weather, transport and external links generate ordinary requests to the relevant official data service or website, which receives your IP address, requested information and normal technical metadata. Those services process requests under their own policies. The app includes no advertising tracking, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioural analytics SDK.

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about website visits under its Privacy Statement. This page adds no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies.

Sharing you choose, app stores and feedback

Exported GPX files, images and assistance text can contain location, time and private markers you explicitly choose to include. Review the content and privacy options, such as hiding track endpoints, before sharing. You choose the receiving app or person; their handling is governed by their own policies, and recipients may retain copies.

Apple (App Store/TestFlight) and Google (Google Play) process downloads, installation and updates, and may provide the developer with aggregated download, crash and diagnostic information according to your device settings. See Apple’s TestFlight & Privacy notice and the Google Privacy Policy. The app itself includes no crash-reporting or analytics SDK. Information you actively send by email, TestFlight or store reviews is used for support, investigating problems and improving the app. Avoid including unnecessary private locations or other people’s information in feedback.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until you delete them in the app or remove them through iOS/Android app-data controls (for example by deleting the app or clearing its storage). Exported files, recipient copies, system backups and Keychain information may be retained differently. Deleting an in-app record does not also delete those copies. Your Apple/iOS or Google/Android settings control device backup and restoration; Android system backup includes only the app database and display preferences.

To ask about, correct or request deletion of support information you have actively supplied to the developer, email takii.hor@gmail.com. We cannot directly read or remotely delete tracks stored only on your device. Information held by Apple, Google, GitHub or other services is also subject to their own controls and retention rules.

Policy updates

We will update this page and its date when data uses or services change. Changes involving new collection or sharing will be explained, with the necessary choices, before those features are enabled.
