本政策適用於 Taki Hor 提供的「山野足跡 / HikeHK」iOS TestFlight 測試版（0.1.0）。英國英文商店名稱為「Hiking Routes & Offline Maps」。私隱及資料查詢請電郵 takii.hor@gmail.com。

此測試版無需帳戶，沒有廣告或第三方行為分析 SDK。路線資料只供瀏覽，尚未實地核實，路線導航保持關閉。正式安全後台及聯絡人逾時通知服務未啟用。

位置、軌跡及本機資料

取得你的同意及系統位置權限後，App 可在地圖顯示位置，並在你主動開始記錄時處理 GPS 座標、時間、精確度、速度、海拔及記錄統計。進行中的記錄可在 App 轉到背景或螢幕關閉時繼續定位。你可以暫停或結束記錄，並隨時在 iOS 設定更改位置權限；拒絕權限後仍可瀏覽路線。

軌跡、匯入的 GPX、私人標記及備註、收藏、偏好和手動公告筆記保存在裝置的 App 儲存空間。部分本機安全設定或綁定資料可保存在 iOS Keychain。此版本沒有由我們營運的軌跡雲端同步，亦不會把完整軌跡自動上傳給開發者。

聯絡人及安全功能

你可以自行輸入或透過系統聯絡人選擇器選取一個電話號碼；App 不會讀取或上傳整個通訊錄。此測試版沒有已啟用的正式安全後台。建立本機安全計劃或排入離線要求，不代表服務已接受行程或聯絡人已收到通知。請勿依賴此測試版提供救援或通知送達保證；App 不會自動報警。

若日後啟用遠端行程分享或安全服務，我們會在啟用前更新本政策，說明供應商、處理資料、所在地、保留安排及選擇，並按需要取得同意。

網絡請求及第三方服務

本測試版內置概覽及六區詳細地圖。使用天氣、交通或外部連結等需連線功能時，對應的官方資料服務或網站會收到正常網絡請求，包括 IP 位址、所要求的資料及一般技術資訊。這些服務按其各自的政策處理請求。我們沒有加入廣告追蹤、跨 App 追蹤或第三方行為分析 SDK。

本政策頁由 GitHub Pages 託管。GitHub 可能按其私隱聲明處理到訪這個網站的技術資訊。此頁沒有額外加入分析工具、廣告、追蹤像素或 Cookie。

你選擇的分享及 TestFlight 回報

匯出 GPX、圖片或求助文字時，資料可能包含位置、時間及你明確選擇包括的私人標記。請先檢查內容及隱藏軌跡兩端等私隱選項；接收資料的 App 或人士由你選擇，並按其政策處理。分享資料可能被接收者另存。

Apple 透過 TestFlight 處理測試安裝、診斷、當機資料及你選擇提交的回報，並可能向開發者提供測試資訊。請參閱 Apple 的 TestFlight 與私隱說明。你透過電郵或 TestFlight 主動提供的資料只用於支援、調查問題及改善此 App；請避免在回報中附上不必要的私人位置或其他人的資料。

保留、刪除及你的選擇

本機記錄會保留至你在 App 中刪除，或按 iOS 的 App 資料管理方式移除。已匯出的檔案、接收者保存的副本、系統備份及 Keychain 資料可能有不同的保存方式；刪除 App 內的記錄不會同時刪除這些副本。裝置備份與還原由你的 Apple/iOS 設定控制。

如要查詢、更正或要求刪除你曾主動提供給開發者的支援資料，請聯絡 takii.hor@gmail.com。我們無法直接讀取或遠端刪除只保存在你裝置上的軌跡。Apple、GitHub 或其他服務持有的資料，亦受其各自的控制及保留規則約束。

政策更新

我們會在資料用途或服務改變時更新本頁及上方日期。若改變涉及新的資料收集或分享，會在啟用前提供相應說明及所需選擇。

English

This policy covers the iOS TestFlight beta of 山野足跡 / HikeHK (version 0.1.0), provided by Taki Hor. Its UK English store name is “Hiking Routes & Offline Maps”. For privacy and data requests, contact takii.hor@gmail.com.

No account is required. This beta contains no advertising or third-party behavioural analytics SDK. Route previews are not field verified and route navigation remains disabled. A production safety backend and overdue contact notifications are not enabled.

Location, tracks and on-device information

With your consent and iOS location permission, the app can show your position on a map and process GPS coordinates, time, accuracy, speed, altitude and recording statistics when you start a recording. An active recording can continue using location in the background or with the screen off. You can pause or finish recording and change location permission in iOS Settings. Browsing remains available without location permission.

Tracks, imported GPX files, private markers and notes, favourites, preferences and manual notice notes are stored in the app’s on-device storage. Some local safety settings or binding information can be stored in iOS Keychain. This version has no developer-operated track cloud sync and does not automatically upload your complete tracks to the developer.

Contacts and safety features

You can enter one phone number or select one through the system contact picker. The app does not read or upload your entire address book. This beta has no enabled production safety backend. Creating a local safety plan or queuing an offline request does not establish server acceptance or notification delivery. Do not rely on the beta for rescue or guaranteed delivery of notifications. The app does not automatically call emergency services.

Before enabling a remote trip-sharing or safety service in a later version, we will update this policy with the providers, data processed, processing locations, retention arrangements and choices, and obtain consent where needed.

Network requests and third-party services

The beta bundles an overview map and detailed previews for six regions. Connected features such as weather, transport and external links generate ordinary requests to the relevant official data service or website, which receives your IP address, requested information and normal technical metadata. Those services process requests under their own policies. The app includes no advertising tracking, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioural analytics SDK.

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about website visits under its Privacy Statement. This page adds no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies.

Sharing you choose and TestFlight feedback

Exported GPX files, images and assistance text can contain location, time and private markers you explicitly choose to include. Review the content and privacy options, such as hiding track endpoints, before sharing. You choose the receiving app or person; their handling is governed by their own policies, and recipients may retain copies.

Apple processes beta installation, diagnostics, crash information and feedback you choose to submit through TestFlight, and may make testing information available to the developer. See Apple’s TestFlight & Privacy notice. Information you actively send by email or TestFlight is used for support, investigating problems and improving the app. Avoid including unnecessary private locations or other people’s information in feedback.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until you delete them in the app or remove them through iOS app-data controls. Exported files, recipient copies, system backups and Keychain information may be retained differently. Deleting an in-app record does not also delete those copies. Your Apple/iOS settings control device backup and restoration.

To ask about, correct or request deletion of support information you have actively supplied to the developer, email takii.hor@gmail.com. We cannot directly read or remotely delete tracks stored only on your device. Information held by Apple, GitHub or other services is also subject to their own controls and retention rules.

Policy updates

We will update this page and its date when data uses or services change. Changes involving new collection or sharing will be explained, with the necessary choices, before those features are enabled.
