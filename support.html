繁體中文

問題、錯誤回報或資料查詢請電郵 takii.hor@gmail.com。請附上裝置型號、iOS／Android 版本、App 版本（設定 › 關於）、重現步驟，以及預期和實際結果。請不要附上不必要的私人位置。

緊急情況請直接致電 999。App 不會自動報警或通知任何人，亦不保證位置準確或資料最新。

為甚麼不能開始路線導航？

路線資料來自漁農自然護理署（漁護署）公開資料，尚未經實地核實，因此只供瀏覽，導航保持關閉。路線開放情況一律顯示為未知；出發前請查閱漁護署及香港天文台的最新資訊。

離線地圖需要下載嗎？

不用。概覽及六區詳細地形圖已內置於 App，可在飛行模式使用。

熄屏後會繼續記錄嗎？

開始記錄並允許位置權限後，可在背景或熄屏時繼續定位，直至你暫停或結束。如軌跡出現缺口，請確認定位服務及 App 權限已開啟、記錄未暫停，並留意衛星接收情況。Android 另須檢查針對本 App 的電池限制。

如何匯出或刪除我的軌跡？

在「軌跡」打開軌跡，可分享 GPX（可選擇隱藏兩端）或刪除。本機軌跡會隨 App 資料移除；已匯出的檔案、接收者副本及系統備份須另行管理。

iOS 如何設定本機提醒？

前往「軌跡 › 設定 › 本機提醒」，選擇未來的日期及時間，按「儲存提醒」並允許通知。你可在同一畫面更改或取消提醒。提醒不要求聯絡人、不傳送位置，也不會確認你是否安全或通知任何人。專注模式、通知設定及裝置關機可能令提醒靜音或延遲，請自行與可信任的人安排報平安。

沒有網絡時如何找到支援及授權？

iOS 的「軌跡 › 設定 › 使用說明及聯絡」內置開發者電郵及功能說明；「資料來源及授權」提供離線法律聲明。寄送電郵及開啟外部政策頁仍需相應服務及網絡。

For questions, bug reports or data requests, email takii.hor@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS/Android version, app version (Settings › About), steps to reproduce, and expected and actual results. Avoid including unnecessary private locations.

In an emergency, call 999 directly. The app never calls or notifies anyone automatically and does not guarantee accurate or current information.

Why can't I start route navigation?

Routes come from public Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department (AFCD) data and have not been field verified, so they are for browsing only and navigation stays disabled. Access status is always shown as unknown; check the latest AFCD and Hong Kong Observatory information before you set out.

Do I need to download offline maps?

No. The overview map and detailed terrain for six regions are built into the app and work in airplane mode.

Does recording continue with the screen off?

After you start a recording and allow location access, recording can continue in the background or with the screen off until you pause or finish. If a track has gaps, check location services and app permission, that recording is not paused, and satellite reception. On Android, also check battery restrictions for the app.

How do I export or delete my tracks?

Open a track under Tracks to share it as GPX (optionally hiding both ends) or delete it. Removing app data removes local tracks; exported files, recipient copies and system backups must be managed separately.

How do I set an on-device reminder on iOS?

Open Tracks › Settings › On-device reminder, choose a future date and time, tap Save reminder and allow notifications. Return to the same screen to change or cancel it. A reminder needs no contact, sends no location, and does not check that you are safe or notify anyone else. Focus, notification settings or a powered-off device may silence or delay it. Arrange your own check-in with someone you trust.

Can I find support and licences offline?

On iOS, Tracks › Settings › Help and contact includes the developer's email address and help text. Data sources and licences includes bundled legal notices. Sending email and opening external policy pages still need the relevant services and connectivity.
